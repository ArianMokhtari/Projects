# Execution scope

## Independently repeated in this replay

- SHA-256 and length checks for the embedded inputs.
- Six recorded interval-membership tests per finite-sample repetition.
- Comparison of recorded expected true HCR risk with the recorded robust bound,
  at tolerance 1e-9, plus a 1e-10 sensitivity recount.
- Explicit denominators for covered, uncovered nonempty and fallback cases.
- Task/method uniqueness and 495-task cell counts for the relevant RGB/cost data.
- Cost scaling and risk = wrong + lambda * abstained + scaled acquisition cost
  in all 198,000 cost-sensitivity rows.
- Aggregation and agreement with all 400 stored cost-summary cells.
- Separate aggregation of historical RGB protocols and selected other tables.
- Dependence exceedance flags from recorded risks/bounds; coverage is a stored
  complete-joint-law flag here, not independently reconstructed from joint samples.

## Supplied but not regenerated by this replay

- Saved prior quality and prior-outcome summaries.
- Saved coupled-policy audit summary; state-level CSV is supplied separately.
- The risk values, policies, intervals, runtimes and memory measurements that
  were generated in the original experiments.
- Seven archived source notebooks, with outputs/metadata cleared and documentation edits and the explicit 0.1.2 corrections recorded in `docs/CORRECTION_RECORD.json`.

## Outside the validated path

- Fresh optimization, exact policy-risk evaluation or calibration-data generation.
- Full RGB annotation/calibration reconstruction or fresh LLM API calls.
- Proving that the archived source version generated every final result file.
- Verified exact source identity of the final-v3 cost sweep. Its numerical outputs
  are included and verified as saved-output arithmetic; a differently labelled
  source version is not relabelled as the exact final source.
- Replaying the assessor-sensitivity study (Issue 10) or LCR-related study
  (Issue 11). Their scientific conclusions are unaffected; they are not exercised
  by this selected package.
- Every final manuscript table, bootstrap interval or figure. The provided figure
  regeneration is the finite-sample observed-coverage plot in the replay notebook.

Changing historical RGB scoring would define a new analysis. The package keeps
historical RGB, the later frozen-response cost sweep and prior sensitivity
separate. The coefficients 0.008 and 0.012 are experimental assumptions; they
are not inferred real-world provider prices or measured operational charges.

Success of 98 software/data checks does not validate all structural assumptions,
scorer correctness or the claimed theorem independently of those assumptions.
